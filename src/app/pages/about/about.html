Imagine · Choose · Visualize

OUR INSPIRATION

A name rooted in creativity

The name takes inspiration from Leonardo da Vinci’s Mona Lisa, a celebrated work known for its artistry and lasting influence. Monalisa brings that spirit of visual exploration into your home, helping you try ideas before painting a real wall.

This project is an independent wall-paint visualizer and is not affiliated with the painting, Leonardo da Vinci, or the Louvre.

THE PROJECT

Plan your room, your way

Monalisa Smart Wall Paint Visualizer is a web application for homeowners and interior-design enthusiasts. Upload a room photo, mark the wall areas you want to change, and explore colours and finishes in context.

Built withMongoDB · Express · Angular · Node.js

MADE FOR EXPERIMENTING

From first idea to room preview

Shape the details and see how they work together in your space.

01

Mark your walls

Upload a room image and outline wall areas with the polygon tool. Adjust corners for a closer fit.

02

Explore colour and finish

Try a colour palette alongside matte, satin, glossy, or textured paint finishes.

03

Try a different mood

Experiment with decorative themes, from geometric and botanical looks to cozy or vintage styles.

04

Keep and share your work

Save designs to your account, reopen them later, compare before and after, or download a preview.
