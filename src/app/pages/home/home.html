A CREATIVE SPACE BY MONALISA PAINTS

Welcome to Monalisa Paints

Inspired by the Mona Lisa's place in art history, this project invites you to explore color in your own space. This is a wall-paint visualizer, not a wall-poster maker: outline a real wall in your room photo, then preview paint colors, finishes, and decorative wall treatments directly on that surface.

The Mona Lisa: art, history, and inspiration

Leonardo da Vinci began the portrait around 1503, and it is widely believed to depict Lisa Gherardini. Its soft sfumato shading gives the painting its distinctive atmosphere. After Leonardo's death, it entered the French royal collection and is now at the Louvre; its 1911 theft and recovery in 1914 helped make it a worldwide icon. That history of art and imagination inspired the name of this color-visualization project.

The woman nicknamed “Monalisa” in reports from the 2025 Kumbh Mela is a separate contemporary person; the shared name is a nickname, not a connection to Leonardo's portrait.

Your preview is a guide—room lighting, wall texture, and screen settings can change how paint looks in real life.
